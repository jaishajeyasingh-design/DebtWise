System Workflow

The FinShield End-to-End Decision Architecture

A closed-loop pipeline combining machine learning, deterministic financial rules, and strict human safety boundaries.

01 • EARLY DISTRESS TRIGGER

Monitors high-frequency transactional telemetry (overdrafts, savings drawdown, cash-flow velocity) before missed payments occur.

02 • AI ROOT-CAUSE DIAGNOSIS

XGBoost classifies distress into 5 archetypes: Income Shock, Debt Overload, Cash-Flow Mismatch, Expense Shock, or Structural Distress.

03 • SUSTAINABLE CAPACITY

Deterministic formula: Income minus essential living cost floor and buffer to establish the exact maximum affordable monthly EMI.

04 • ELIGIBILITY & SAFETY FILTER

Hard safety constraints actively reject predatory or inappropriate options (e.g. debt consolidation rejected if debt-overloaded).

05 • EXPLAINABILITY & CONSENT

SHAP feature attribution + Claude LLM plain-English translation. Borrower reviews options, trade-offs, and grants explicit consent.

06 • SAFE DUAL-EXECUTION

Tier A low-risk reversible actions execute automatically; Tier B/C contractual modifications route to human hardship officers.

Action Hierarchy

The 7-Level Intervention Ladder

Ordered strictly by intrusiveness and reversibility — preventing premature or aggressive contractual restructuring.

L0 MONITOR Passive telemetry tracking when financial metrics are within safe variance limits. Zero customer contact. ZERO FRICTION
L1 INFORM Actionable spending alerts, obligation calendar sync, proactive financial coaching tips. INFORMATIONAL
L2 TIMING FIX Align EMI dates with actual payroll deposit, autopay re-timing, fee/overdraft relief waivers. 100% REVERSIBLE
L3 REPAYMENT PLAN Personalized 3-6 month catch-up schedule with stabilized, budget-friendly installments. AFFORDABLE
L4 TEMPORARY RELIEF 1-3 month payment moratorium, interest freeze, or temporary reduction to capacity floor. SHORT-TERM
L5 FORMAL SUPPORT Tenor extension, structured loan restructuring, contractual interest rate reduction. CONTRACTUAL
L6 HUMAN ESCALATION Hardship team review for highly complex, vulnerable, or disputed borrower situations. HUMAN JUDGMENT
